Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Pomly is built to be private. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Without Sync, nothing leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Your timer, settings and focus history, stored by the system on your device. Widgets and the Watch read them from your device only.

Sync (optional)

If you turn on Sync Devices, Pomly creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase (no name, email or phone number) and stores, for your paired devices only:

  • an anonymous ID for each device;
  • the current timer state (phase, length, start and end time, label);
  • your focus sessions (start time, duration, label, completed or ended early);
  • a temporary 6-character pairing code that expires after 10 minutes.

Database rules let only the paired devices read or write this data, over encrypted HTTPS. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising. Tap Stop Syncing to leave; reset your history to delete synced sessions.

Permissions

Alarms and notifications are used only to tell you a session ended. Siri and Shortcuts run only when you ask.

Children

Pomly collects no personal information and is suitable for all ages.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub.
