Questions

Good to know.

Is Pomly really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to unlock. Pomly is a passion project, and it's free for everyone.

Will the alarm ring if my phone is on silent?

Yes. Pomly uses the same alarm system as Apple's Clock app, so it rings through Silent mode and Focus, and you can start your break right from the alert.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open Pomly and start focusing. If you'd like your devices to stay in sync, you pair them with a short code shown on screen. No email, no password.

What does Pomly know about me?

Nothing it doesn't need. There's no tracking or analytics. If you turn on sync, only your timer and focus history are shared, and only between your own devices. Read the privacy policy.

Why isn't it on the App Store?

Pomly is made by one independent developer. Until it's listed there, the steps above let you install it free, and it keeps itself updated.

My Mac says Pomly is from an unidentified developer.

That's expected for new independent apps. Right-click Pomly in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.
