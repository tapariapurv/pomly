Everything a focus timer should be

Simple

One drag to set

Drag around the dial for 1–120 minutes or tap 15 · 25 · 45 · 60. Start becomes Pause and End in one fluid Liquid Glass morph.

Reliable

Rings through Silent

Alarms break through Silent mode and Focus, with "Take a Break" right on the alert.

Glanceable

Live Activities & widgets

Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, StandBy, Home Screen widgets that start sessions, Control Center and Siri.

Mac

A menu bar timer you drag

Press the menu bar icon and drag down to pick a length. A floating mini timer and ⌃⌥P / ⌃⌥D shortcuts work from any app.

Watch

Your wrist, your dial

An edge-to-edge dial with the Digital Crown, plus watch-face complications and the Smart Stack.

Sync

Every device, one timer

Pair with a 6-character code. Start on your Mac, pause on your Watch. No sign-up.

Questions

Is Pomly really free?

Yes. It's MIT-licensed and open source, with no ads, subscriptions, analytics or tracking.

What does sync store?

Only if you turn it on: an anonymous ID, the current timer, and your focus sessions (times and labels), readable only by your paired devices. See the privacy policy.

Why does macOS say the app is from an unidentified developer?

Pomly isn't notarized yet. The Homebrew cask and install script clear the quarantine flag for you, or right-click Pomly and choose Open.

Which systems does it need?

iOS, iPadOS, macOS and watchOS 26 or later.
