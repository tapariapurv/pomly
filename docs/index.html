Questions

Good to know.

Is Pomly really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, nothing to unlock. Pomly is a passion project, and it's free for everyone.

Will the alarm ring if my phone is on silent?

Yes. Pomly uses the same alarm system as Apple's Clock app, so it rings through Silent mode and Focus, and you can start your break right from the alert.

Do I need an account?

No. Open Pomly and start. To keep your devices in sync, pair them with a short code shown on screen. No email, no password.

What does Pomly know about me?

Nothing it doesn't need. There's no tracking or analytics. If you turn on sync, only your timer and focus history are shared, and only between your own devices. Read the privacy policy.

My Mac says it can't check Pomly for malicious software.

That's expected for independent apps that aren't notarized yet. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once, and the one-line Terminal install skips it.

Why isn't it on the App Store?

Pomly is made by one independent developer. Until it's listed, the steps above install it free, and it keeps itself updated.
