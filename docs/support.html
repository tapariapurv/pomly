Support

Sync my devices

On one device open Settings › Sync Devices (Watch: the sync button; Mac: the panel). On the other, type the 6-character code. Codes last 10 minutes.

The alarm did not ring

Allow Alarms when asked on the first Start, or in Settings › Pomly. If alarms are off, Pomly falls back to a notification.

Mac menu bar

  • Click the icon for the panel; press and drag away from it to pick a length.
  • ⌃⌥P start or pause and ⌃⌥D drag mode, from any app.
  • Timer › Mini Timer (⇧⌘M) floats a small dial you can drag anywhere.
  • Don’t see the icon? A menu bar manager may be hiding it, or there may be no room by the notch.

“Can’t check for malicious software” on Mac

Pomly isn’t notarized yet. The first time you open it, click Done, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Installing with the one-line Terminal command or Homebrew skips this step.
